# Animal-Protection-Bill

The Animal Protection Bill, which was approved by the Scientific Association for Environmental Education and Sustainable Development.
Animal Protection Bill

The Animal Protection Bill was approved by the Council of Ministers in June 2021 and was sent to the Parliament by the President on August 6, 2021, for further legal procedures.

Justification:
Considering the necessity of ethical and legal protection of animal life, health, and security based on religious teachings, 
as well as the need to regulate animals, especially stray and abandoned animals, prevent animal cruelty, and keep society away 
from violent behaviors towards various animal species, and to promote proper ways of interacting with animals, 
this bill is presented to fill legal gaps in combating animal cruelty and to introduce preventive and deterrent legal measures.
This bill has been approved by the Ethics Committee of the Scientific Society. Chapter 5 of this bill, which deals with work with laboratory animals, is as follows. This bill is available in its entirety in the Society's archives and can be sent upon request.

[Chapter Five -- Management of Laboratory Animals]

Article 30: The use of animals for scientific purposes is subject to obtaining the necessary permits, specifying the species of laboratory animals in the permit, in accordance with relevant laws and regulations.
Note: The health conditions and regulations for keeping laboratory animals, considering their basic needs, will be determined by the Veterinary Organization.
Article 31: Scientific centers subject to Article 30 of this law are required to examine alternative methods for the use of laboratory animals in their activities, as follows, and prioritize them if possible:
1.	Using animal simulators.
2.	Computer-based methods and mathematical and statistical calculations.
3.	Using lower-level living organisms.
Note: Reducing the number of animals to an acceptable level using various methods should not increase the pain and suffering of the animals used and should not compromise the scientific validity of the results.
Article 32: Scientific activities on laboratory animals must be conducted in compliance with the following:
1.	Scientific quality in performing the work by individuals with the necessary expertise and qualifications and holding the necessary permits from relevant authorities.
2.	Observing ethical research principles regarding animals.
3.	Compliance of drugs, equipment, and tools used with relevant standards.
4.	Performing operations in a way that does not cause unnecessary pain and suffering to animals and does not lead to the repetition of experiments.
Article 33: For teaching various skills, including dissection in medical and veterinary schools, schools, and other research and educational centers, in addition to the necessity of using alternative tools
Article 33: For the purpose of teaching various skills, including dissection in medical and veterinary schools, as well as in schools and other research centers, in addition to the necessity of using alternative tools, only the carcasses of owned animals and animals that have died due to natural causes, accidents, severe injuries, and non-communicable diseases shall be used. Stray animals may be used only when necessary.
Chapter Six - Animal Cruelty
Article 34: Any intentional act that causes harm, injury, or distress to the body or psyche of animals covered by this law is prohibited and shall be punishable under the provisions of this law.
Note: Declaring an animal as wild or impure based on religious rulings does not justify cruelty or abuse towards it.
Article 35: Anyone who intentionally and without necessity, and without observing relevant regulations, kills, poisons, mutilates, or disfigures an animal shall be sentenced to a fine or imprisonment of the seventh degree, considering the severity of the crime, its consequences, and the type of animal involved.
Note: If such actions are accompanied by those mentioned in Article 41 of this law, the offender shall be subject to a more severe punishment by one degree.
